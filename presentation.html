The idea

A game people already understand.

Make the rules familiar, then make every decision feel immediate, visible, and rewarding.

Instead of overwhelming players with instructions, the interface gives them a clear loop: choose a challenge, read the clue, guess a letter, and react to the result.

The gameplay loop

Four moves from setup to victory.

01 / SETUP

Choose the arena

Pick categories, word count, and difficulty before the round begins.

02 / READ

Find the clue

Use the category and optional hint to build a mental shortlist.

03 / GUESS

Commit a letter

Click the on-screen keyboard or type directly from a physical keyboard.

04 / REPEAT

Chase the win

Complete every word before ten mistakes close the round.

Built for replay

Small details make the loop stick.

⌁

Eight categories

From programming languages to fruits, every round can feel different.

◈

Three difficulty levels

Adjust the pressure with Easy, Medium, or High challenge modes.

✓

Instant feedback

Correct sounds, error states, drawing stages, and confetti make progress tangible.

◌

Hints when needed

The eye button offers a nudge without taking the decision away from the player.

AR

English + Arabic

Switch the interface language without restarting the game.

☼

Your atmosphere

Choose dark or light mode and keep the experience comfortable.
